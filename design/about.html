How Lungisa works

We introduce you to capable young people from Cape Town’s townships, vouched for by the trainers who know them. Then we stay close to you both for the first 90 days.

  1. 1

    Vouched for by their trainer

    Every candidate has finished a programme with one of our training partners, such as Just Grace in Langa. Their trainer watched them work every day and tells you what they saw. We never pay trainers per hire, so they have no reason to oversell anyone.

  2. 2

    You interview, you decide

    Interviews are free during the pilot. If you hire, they work for you directly, on your terms.

  3. 3

    We stay close for 90 days

    We check in with you and your new starter twice a week for the first fortnight, then weekly. Placements usually break early, over things like transport or a rough first week, so that’s where we put the most effort. You can pause your own check-ins; theirs carry on.

    Each stitch is a check-in. The dense start is the first fortnight; the pink disc is day 90.

Why Lungisa

Lungisa means to make right in isiXhosa. The people we introduce don’t need fixing. What needs fixing is a hiring system that runs on networks they were never part of.
